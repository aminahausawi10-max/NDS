About Nigeria Diaspora Services (NDS)

Nigeria Diaspora Services (NDS) is an independent documentation assistant committed to bridging the gap for Nigerians living abroad.

Navigating bureaucratic government documentation processes from another country can be stress-inducing. Our professional support network in Nigeria coordinates validation, verification, fresh submissions, and legalization on your behalf with utmost security and transparency.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted documentation assistance and diaspora support platform connecting Nigerians worldwide to essential services.

Our Mission

To provide reliable, transparent, and professional documentation processing assistance and support to Nigerians in the Diaspora.
